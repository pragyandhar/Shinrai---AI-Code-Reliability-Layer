Any idea that comes up to mind can be a possible solution but is it the best solution? That is a big question. You must analyze all the possible ways to break your original idea, if it remains unbreakable then CONGRATULATIONS you got the best idea but if not then sorry your idea SUCKS and you need to find a better idea to fit into the scenario and the context and the challenge.
